# Metal-Content-of-Consumer-Products-Tested
The “Metal Content of Consumer Products Tested” dataset presents a detailed compilation of laboratory-tested consumer goods, focusing on the presence and concentration of various metals such as lead and others that may pose potential health risks. This dataset is designed to support analytical exploration into the safety and regulatory compliance of everyday products across multiple countries and product categories.
The data includes critical fields such as metal type, concentration levels, product types (like cosmetics, dietary supplements and food items), country of manufacture, and purchase location. It offers a comprehensive view into the origin and safety status of products used globally, making it a valuable resource for quality control professionals, health and safety researchers, and regulatory bodies.
The interactive Excel dashboard developed for this project leverages pivot tables, slicers, bar and line charts, heatmaps, and pie charts to provide dynamic visual insights. Users can filter by product type, country, and metal concentration to identify patterns, detect outliers, and draw meaningful conclusions. This project demonstrates how conventional spreadsheet tools can be transformed into powerful instruments for public safety monitoring and product quality assurance.
